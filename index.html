The short version

I like taking a model from a research idea to something that actually runs in front of people — training it, wiring it into a backend, and deploying it. That has meant Nepali speech recognition, LLM document QA, image tagging, and the AWS plumbing that keeps them all alive.

The same week might involve fine-tuning a transformer and fixing the CI/CD pipeline that serves it. I am happiest when both are on the table.
